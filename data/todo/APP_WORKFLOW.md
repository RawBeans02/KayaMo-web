# Daily workflow

## Product principle

The user can always add, lock, move, skip, or ignore a proposal. AI fills gaps
in a frame the user started — it does not replace the frame unless asked.

## End-to-end

1. **Capture** — type, talk, or Brain Dump into Inbox. Classification can wait.
2. **Frame** — place FIXED events (class, appointments). Optionally lock them.
3. **Intent** — capacity + Day Intent (already on `daily_plans`).
4. **Plan My Day** — Mus proposes; user Accept / Edit / Replan.
5. **Execute** — timeline + FIXED / FLEXIBLE / ANYTIME lists. Drag is advisory.
6. **Live change** — Replan remaining day from *now*. Past stays recorded.
7. **Review** — evening: completed, moved, skipped (no shame copy).
8. **Learn** — store actual durations for next estimates. Suggestions only.

## Conversational capture

User: "Remind me to email Professor Chris tomorrow morning."

Mus proposes a TASK with preferred window morning, date tomorrow. User confirms.
Mus does not write until confirm.

Brain Dump may create several inbox rows. Ask only where a hard time matters.

## Cross-module

Gym, meals, and weather may *suggest* moves. They never auto-write calorie
targets or invent medical advice. Food-aware planning is opt-in.

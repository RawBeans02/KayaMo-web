## The service

KayaMo helps you organize everyday actions and goals and record food and workouts.
It is a general wellness and personal-organization tool, not medical care, a diagnosis,
an emergency service or a promise of particular health, weight or fitness results.

## Accounts and your information

Keep your password to yourself and keep access to your sign-in email secure. You are
responsible for information you choose to enter and for checking the outcome of
changes. Demo data stays in its browser and does not automatically transfer when
you sign in. Offline or unsynced work may be lost if browser data is cleared.

## Nutrition and exercise

Nutrition sources, portions, labels and matches can be incomplete or inaccurate.
Review the source, food identity and quantity before saving. Do not rely on KayaMo
as the sole basis for medical, allergy, dietary-treatment or exercise-safety decisions.
Consult an appropriately qualified professional when personalized advice is needed.
Stop an activity and seek appropriate help if you experience concerning symptoms.

## Mus and AI-assisted features

AI outputs may be wrong or incomplete. Review proposals before confirming changes.
Grant access only to context you want used. AI availability may be limited by provider
availability and request allowances. Do not use Mus for emergencies or diagnoses.

## Acceptable use

Do not access another person's account, bypass access controls or request limits,
submit malicious content, disrupt the service, or misuse another person's information.
Report suspected security problems privately to **help.kayamo@gmail.com**.

## Your content and privacy

You retain your rights in content you provide. KayaMo needs permission to process
that content to provide the features you request, subject to the approved privacy
notice and your available controls. This is not an unrestricted right to sell your
health information or use it for unrelated purposes.

## Availability, changes and support

Features may be unavailable during failures or maintenance. We do not guarantee
uninterrupted access or recovery of every unsynced/demo entry. Support is available
at **help.kayamo@gmail.com**; no 24/7 or emergency response is promised.

Contact support for account/privacy requests. Do not imply that signing out deletes
an account or that an automated export/deletion flow exists where it does not.

# KayaMo accessibility statement — draft, not published

Publication prerequisites: owner-approved support contact, review date and wording.
Do not publish this draft with placeholders or imply certification/Apple approval.

## Our aim

We want KayaMo to be usable by people with a range of abilities and access needs.
Our accessibility target is WCAG 2.2 Level AA. This is a target, not a claim that
every criterion has been independently verified or met.

## Available accessibility features

The website includes labeled navigation, keyboard-accessible controls and visible
focus states, light/dark/system appearance, reduced-transparency controls, and
reduced-motion support. Core screens support narrow browser layouts.

## Testing and known limitations

We test core screens with automated accessibility checks and keyboard/regression
tests. Automated tests cannot establish complete accessibility. Real screen-reader
speech output, native browser zoom and the final hosted release still need review.
Third-party sign-in and data-service behavior also require live verification.

This statement must be updated after the final Claude Design is implemented and
tested; current evidence lives in the botanical accessibility/state matrix.

## Feedback and assistance

Email **help.kayamo@gmail.com** with the subject **KayaMo accessibility**.
Support is handled directly by KayaMo's operator. No emergency or 24/7 response is promised.

When reporting an issue, describe the page, what you were trying to do, and the
browser or assistive technology if you wish. Do not send passwords, sign-in links,
API keys or personal health records. Screenshots are optional; remove private data.
